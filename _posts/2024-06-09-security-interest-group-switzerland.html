Security Interest Group Switzerland is a peer-to-peer organization dedicated to helping cyber security, IT risk management, privacy, and compliance professionals be more successful. We accomplish this mission by providing opportunities for those professionals to meet periodically or to communicate through LinkedIn groups to share with one another and hear from industry experts.

The greatest advantage of this group is its independence from 3rd parties. New ideas can easily be realized without the need to follow rules or guidelines. If they provide value to the goals of SIGS and the community, we will realize them!

We are  always searching for additional security freaks which are interested to get involved in the organization and would like to support the know how exchange. All ideas and suggestions to improve or enhance these platforms are very welcome! Because we are sure that know how sharing and information exchange is a very important factor for the future and the upcoming challenges.

For more information, check out at https://www.sig-switzerland.ch
