Swiss Cyber Storm is the long-established security conference that sets the IT security agenda for the Swiss security industry. We present renowned international speakers to a national audience. At the event, international experts present the latest findings to a diverse national audience ranging from students and techies to IT security specialists and CISOs. While other conferences like to talk about vulnerabilities, Swiss Cyber Storm provides context and presents practical and proven solutions to structural security problems.

The conference is organized by Swiss Cyber Storm, a non-profit organization. Swiss Cyber Storm also runs the Swiss part of the European Cyber Security Challenges, with a focus on identifying and supporting young cyber talent.

For more information, please visit https://www.swisscyberstorm.com
