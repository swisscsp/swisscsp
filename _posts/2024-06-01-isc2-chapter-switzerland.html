Welcome to the ISC2 Chapter Switzerland

The ISC2 Chapter Switzerland promotes the community and networks specialists for information security who are resident or working in Switzerland or who have close ties to Switzerland. Our mission is to advance information security in a local community by providing our members and other security professionals with the opportunity to share knowledge, grow professionally, educate others, and collaborate on projects.

Get to know the Chapter

Are you interested in discussing hot topics in information and application security, networking with peers and learning from industry experts, all while earning CPE credits? Attend one of our meetings and industry events to meet the chapter and learn who we are! See Events.
